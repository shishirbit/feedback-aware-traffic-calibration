# Frontiers of Computer Science manuscript package

Import `springer-overleaf.zip` into Overleaf. Choose **pdfLaTeX** and `main.tex`, then recompile from scratch. The package contains exactly one TeX file, the author-supplied Springer class, an inline numbered bibliography and eight vector PDF figures. No BibTeX step is required.

The supplied 2025 Frontiers of Computer Science instructions have been applied. The manuscript has a 201-word one-paragraph abstract, six semicolon-separated key words, a short running head, definitions and nomenclature, 21 three-line tables, eight labelled figures, two algorithms, acknowledgements/competing interests, reproducibility information and two short author biographies. Reported results and registered claim boundaries are retained.

`fcs-guideline-audit.md` gives the requirement-by-requirement check and remaining author actions. Both authors' genuine photographs are still needed for their biography panels; no placeholders or fabricated photos are present.

Separate journal items: `fcs-highlights.pptx` is a three-slide presentation and is not included in the article PDF. `fcs-figure-artwork.zip` contains eight 600-dpi TIFF files for the journal's source-artwork requirement. Both are in the repository's manuscript directory; the highlights PPTX is also included under `submission/` inside the Overleaf ZIP.

Both authors share Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Manipal University Jaipur, Jaipur, Rajasthan 303007, India. Satyabrata Roy is the corresponding author. The 26 references remain original published papers; `references.bib` is editable source metadata while the compiled reference list is embedded in `main.tex`.
